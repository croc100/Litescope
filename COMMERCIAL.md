# Commercial licensing

Litescope is open source under the **GNU AGPL-3.0** (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)).
You can use, modify, and self-host it freely. The AGPL's one condition: if you
run a modified version as a network service, you must make your source
modifications available to its users.

For organizations that can't accept those terms, a **commercial license** is
available. It is the same software, offered under different terms.

## When you need a commercial license

- You want to **embed or redistribute** Litescope inside a closed-source product.
- You **modify** Litescope and offer it as a hosted/network service without
  publishing your changes (an AGPL exception).
- Your legal or procurement policy **disallows AGPL** dependencies.
- You want a **support SLA**, security response commitments, or an invoice and
  contract rather than a community best-effort.

You do **not** need a commercial license to:

- Use the CLI, the local `litescope serve` dashboard, or self-host internally.
- Run Litescope in CI, including the [GitHub Action](action.yml).
- Build on it under the AGPL and share your modifications.

## What's included

- A perpetual, non-AGPL license grant for the agreed use.
- Priority support and a security-response commitment.
- Optional: a self-host deployment of the [hosted dashboard](https://litescope-site.pages.dev/#pricing)
  on your own infrastructure (air-gapped / on-prem).
